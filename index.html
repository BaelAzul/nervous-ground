Ready when you are

This isn't helping. Take me somewhere quiet.

Around six breaths a minute is a pace many people find settling. It's a direction to explore, not a test, and there's no right number.

Some days your body will happily slow down. Other days it won't, and that's useful to notice, not a failure. Start where you are, go as far as feels okay, and stop whenever you like.
